? Infra Control 80f: the phone as remote (remote.html)
  1. Pair the phone with this PC (Windows: Settings › Bluetooth & devices).
  2. Make an incoming COM port: More Bluetooth settings › COM Ports › Add… › Incoming.
  3. Click + Infra Control 80f and choose that COM port. It is remembered, ↻ on the unit chooses again.
  4. On the phone open remote.html in Chrome (Android, Chrome 138 or newer), tap Connect and choose this PC.

The slider on the phone picks remote 1-4. Each remote selects its own loco like a Control 80f. Only one Infra Control can be added.
